4 · Timeline — underground (“The Pipeline”, hand-drawn)

Direction: the timeline is drawn as a sketched sewer, not a diagram. A single wobbly pipe snakes down a hand-inked brick tunnel wall (irregular bricks, moss, damp streaks, hatched shading) from a manhole shaft with a light beam and ladder, and empties out into a murky pool with a rat at the edge. Every line is roughened with an SVG turbulence/displacement filter and double-stroked, so nothing is ruler-straight. Reading it: yellow-lettered START ↓ at the manhole, chevrons on the pipe show the flow, five numbered valve wheels are the milestones, FINISH is the outfall. Each milestone is a riveted tin plate hung beside its valve, slightly tilted, with a hand-lettered yellow date tab. Text lives only on those plates and the title plaque (solid, ≥7.9:1). Pipe highlight strokes animate slowly (reduced-motion: static). Hand-lettering: Permanent Marker for dates/titles only, Inter for body.
